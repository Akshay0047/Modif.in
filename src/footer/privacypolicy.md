# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

MODIF.in ("Modif", "we", "us", or "our") operates the `modif.in` website and its text, image, and document tools (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our Services, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, improve, and secure the Services.

### 1.1 Information You Provide

Every current tool processes the files and text you select or enter locally in your browser. Selecting a file or using a tool does not upload its contents to us or a third-party processing service.

You may separately choose to send us a support request, issue report, or other communication. We receive the information you explicitly include in that message, including any attachments you choose to send through your email or other communication provider.

Current tools do not require an account.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

Your browser requests public static website and tool assets, such as pages, scripts, styles, fonts, processing engines, and OCR language data. Our hosting infrastructure necessarily receives ordinary request information, which may include:

* IP address.
* Browser request headers, such as browser type and version.
* Requested public page or asset path.
* Referring page information, when sent by your browser.
* Request date, time, and response status.

Tool files, entered text, and processing results are not included in these requests. The site does not integrate analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or tracking tags.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected through the Services may be used to:

* Provide, operate, and maintain the Services.
* Improve the design, performance, reliability, and usability of the Services.
* Monitor, diagnose, and prevent technical problems.
* Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, spam, and security incidents.
* Respond to support requests, questions, or other communications.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

We do not receive tool content for processing, training, advertising, or product analytics. Explicit support communications are handled separately to respond to the request you send.

## 3. Local Content and Tool Processing

Tools let you select or enter content, including images, text, spreadsheets, PDFs, DOCX files, CSV files, and other documents.

All current tool processing happens on your device in your browser. Files and text are not sent to MODIF.in servers or third-party processing services. Processing engines, workers, and OCR language data are downloaded as public static assets from the same site; these downloads do not contain your tool content.

Only process content that you are authorized to use.

We do not retain server copies of tool inputs or results because they are not uploaded. Copies you download or otherwise save remain under your control on your device.

## 4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The site uses local browser storage to remember the motion preference. This setting stays on your device and does not include tool inputs or document contents.

The site does not use analytics or advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking integrations.

You can clear local storage through your browser settings; doing so resets saved preferences.

## 5. Analytics and Advertising

The current Services do not integrate Google Analytics, Google AdSense, or other analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 6. Hosting and Communication Providers

The website relies on hosting infrastructure to deliver public static assets. Hosting providers may process ordinary request information to deliver and secure the site.

If you explicitly send a support message, your chosen email or communication provider and ours may process that message. This is separate from using a tool locally in your browser.

These providers have their own privacy policies governing information they process.

## 7. How We Share Information

We may share information with:

* Service providers that help us operate the Services.
* Security and fraud-prevention providers.
* Legal, regulatory, or governmental authorities when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.
* Other parties in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of assets, where permitted by law.

We do not sell personal information merely because you use the Services.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the Services, maintaining security, resolving disputes, complying with legal obligations, and enforcing our agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

This applies to support communications and ordinary hosting request information. Tool inputs and results are not retained on our servers because tool processing stays on your device.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You use the Services at your own risk and should avoid submitting sensitive information unless necessary.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where applicable law requires verified parental consent.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in violation of applicable law, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

## 11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, which may include:

* Requesting access to personal information we hold about you.
* Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
* Requesting deletion of certain information.
* Objecting to or restricting certain processing.
* Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.
* Requesting information about certain third-party processing.

Some requests may be subject to legal or technical limitations.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the contact method provided on the Services.

## 12. International Data Processing

Hosting and communication providers may process ordinary request information or explicit support messages in countries other than the country in which you are located. Tool content is processed locally on your device.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards may be used for international transfers or processing.

## 13. Third-Party Links

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of third-party websites. You should review their privacy policies before providing information to them.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Services, technology, legal requirements, or other operational needs.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Your continued use of the Services after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes acknowledgment of the updated policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## 15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Modif, please contact us through the contact method provided on the Services.
